# RingOrder EPOS

Read **[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)** first — it holds the rules, the layout, and the
list of things that look like rules but are not.

Two habits specific to working here:

**Run the app, do not just build it.** A till is flows. Compiling proves
nothing about whether a shift can be closed or a ticket can be paid.

**Write down what you decided**, in [docs/WORKLOG.md](docs/WORKLOG.md), while you
still remember why. The next session starts cold and will otherwise re-derive
the decision differently.
